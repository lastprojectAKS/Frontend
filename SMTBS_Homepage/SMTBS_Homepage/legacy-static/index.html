Need help?

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? The support links in the footer can point you in the right direction.

How do I book a ticket?

Pick a movie from Now Showing, choose a showtime, then select your seats and confirm. You'll get a booking reference on screen once it's done.

Can I choose my own seat?

Yes. When you book a ticket, an interactive seat map opens so you can pick any available seat in the theatre.

How do loyalty points work?

You earn points on every booking, with select movies offering double points. Points can be redeemed for discounts on future tickets.

Can I cancel or change a booking?

Most bookings can be changed or cancelled up to two hours before showtime. Refunds are credited back to your original payment method.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes, all bookings go through an encrypted, secure checkout process so your payment details stay protected.
